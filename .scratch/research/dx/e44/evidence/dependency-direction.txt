DX-E44 dependency-direction proof
=================================

Command:

  nix develop -c sh -c \
    'dune describe workspace > .scratch/research/dx/e44/evidence/dependency-after.txt'

Relevant `dune describe` records:

  ((name eta)
   (uid 4959d989cd769579373219a9cc44899f)
   (local true)
   (requires ()))

  ((name eta_observability)
   (uid 9590a6e6350c01e87f118721d299baad)
   (local true)
   (requires (4959d989cd769579373219a9cc44899f)))

The SDK's sole required UID is `eta`; root `eta` requires no library. Therefore
the edge is exactly `eta_observability -> eta`, with no root-to-SDK edge and no
cycle. The complete machine-readable before/after workspaces are
`dependency-before.txt` and `dependency-after.txt`.
